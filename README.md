# I AM House

Music / NFT house app with a built-in QR + barcode scanner and Cash App pay links.

**Live (after Pages is on):** https://militiamarie.github.io/iam-app/

## Enable Pages once
1. Open https://github.com/Militiamarie/iam-app/settings/pages
2. Source: Deploy from a branch → `main` / root
3. Save, wait 1–2 minutes, then open the live link.
